# 12. Final homepage: the cut

Prepared by TechnoTaau Team, Design Lead, 5 September 2026, after Jakhar
Singh's review of build 6: the page is too long, the second half has no
images, the form is at the bottom, and it must not get more complicated.

## The reverse-engineering, in one table

What each block does for the client's goal (stay, read, gain confidence,
enquire), and the verdict.

| Block (build 6) | Job | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Hero, one screen | Offer, legacy, one number, two doors | Keep |
| Client strip | Who trusts them | Keep |
| Three ways we work | What they do, in three photos | Keep |
| FDE spotlight | The flagship, with a photo | Keep |
| Programs roster, three rows | The professional's comparison | Keep |
| Who writes to us | Routing, already done twice above | Cut |
| Consulting: point of view and diagram | The proof of understanding | Keep |
| Seven questions block | Curiosity, but a full screen | Cut to one line with the one-pager offer |
| Five practices list | Service links | Move to the footer |
| Built to survive the handover | Method, for a CTO who already reads | Cut (consulting page) |
| Straight answers, six | Objection handling | Keep four |
| Outcomes and mentors | Trust | Keep, add a photograph |
| Insights | SEO and rhythm | Keep, after the form |
| Contact | The conversion | Keep, move up, put it on a photograph |

## Final order

1. Promise bar, header
2. Hero
3. Client strip
4. Three ways we work
5. FDE spotlight
6. Programs
7. Consulting: point of view, before and after, one-pager line
8. Outcomes and mentors, with a photo
9. Straight answers, four
10. Contact, on a photo under the navy scrim
11. Insights
12. Footer, with a consulting column

Target: about 8,000px at 1366 and under 15,000px at 360, from 11,600 and
21,400. Photographs after the halfway point: outcomes, contact, insights.

## What is kept for the inner pages

The persona cards with their first-step lines (contact and program pages),
the seven questions in full (consulting page and the one-pager), the five
practices with their lines (consulting page), the four phases (consulting
page). All in `prototype/copy.md`.
