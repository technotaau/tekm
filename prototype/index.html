CONSULTING

The gap is never the model. It is everything around it.

Most enterprise AI pilots work. Most of them never go live. What stops them is not the model but the parts a demo leaves out: data access with permissions attached, an evaluation set, a deployment path, monitoring, and an engineer who owns the system after the consultants leave.

TEKMentors was built for that gap. We scope the business problem before the architecture, build against your real data, and hand over to a team we have trained to run it. The same practitioners do the consulting and teach the programs, which is why both hold up after the engagement ends.

USE THIS TOMORROW

Seven questions before an AI pilot goes live

Ask these in the next steering meeting. If three of them have no answer, the pilot is not stuck on the model.

  1. Who owns it on Monday morning? A named person, not a team.
  2. What is the baseline number it has to move, and who agreed it?
  3. Where does the data come from when nobody is exporting it by hand?
  4. What is in the evaluation set, and who wrote the failing cases?
  5. What happens when the model is wrong in front of a customer?
  6. Has security seen the design, or only the demo?
  7. What does it cost per thousand requests at real volume?

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The questions we get in almost every first meeting.

Answered the way we would across a table, without the hedging. If yours is missing, it is probably the interesting one, so put it in the form.

Where should we actually start with AI?

With the process your best people complain about most. It is usually unglamorous, already has a number attached, and nobody has offered to fix it because it is boring. That is exactly why it is the right first project.

Our pilot works. Why can it not go live?

Because a pilot proves the model and production needs everything else: governed data access, an evaluation set, a deployment path, monitoring and a named owner on Monday morning. We build those in from week one.

Do we hire AI engineers or train ours?

Train the ones who already know your systems, your data and who to call when something breaks at nine on a Friday. That knowledge takes years to rebuild. The AI part takes weeks, and that is the part we teach.

What does the first conversation cost?

Nothing, including the one page recommendation that follows it. It is the fastest way for both sides to find out whether the work is a fit, and you keep the page either way.

Can you work inside our security rules?

Yes, and we would rather meet your risk and security people in week one than week six. We will sign your NDA before the scoping session if that is easier. Online, on site in Delhi NCR or a mix.

What happens when you leave?

Your engineers keep running it, because they built most of it with us watching rather than the other way round. Handover is a phase with a date on it, not a conversation we have when the invoice is due. If you still need us in a year, something went wrong.
