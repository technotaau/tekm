STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The things people hesitate to ask on a form.

If your question is not here, put it in the form. We would rather answer it now than in month three.

Ask us directly

Will I be put into a sales pipeline?

No. Your details go to the consulting team and stay there. There is no newsletter, no automated sequence, and nobody will call you twice if you have said no once.

What does the first conversation cost?

Nothing, including the one page recommendation that follows it. We do that because it is the fastest way for both sides to find out whether the work is a fit.

Are we too small for you?

Probably not. We have run engagements with a single platform team and with programs spanning several business units. Training cohorts start from around eight people.

Can you work with our data and security rules?

Yes, and we would rather meet your risk and security people in week one than in week six. We will sign your NDA before the scoping session if that is easier.

Do you work remotely, or on site?

Both. We are based in Delhi NCR and work with teams across India, the Gulf and the US, on whatever mix of remote and on site the work actually needs.

Who will actually turn up?

A practitioner with roughly twenty years behind them, and on programs the mentor who will teach your cohort. You will have their name and background before the call, so you can look them up first.
